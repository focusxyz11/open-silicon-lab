# Decisions

Every decision the owner makes about the lab, dated, with its reason, oldest first. Entries are never rewritten: a
reversal is a new entry that names what it reverses. What is true now is in `docs/ROADMAP.md`, `docs/STATUS.md` and
the documents they link to; this file is the history of how it got there. It starts on 26 November 2026; earlier
decisions are recorded in those documents, their dated notes and the history of the repository.

## 26 November 2026

- **The physics-to-intelligence path is the lab's spine.** "Build a thinking machine from physics up, and invent your
  own along the way", in the spirit of Nand to Tetris for the AI era: what you build on one rung is carried up and used
  by the next. Why: one promise anyone can understand, buildable step by step, leading straight to the next
  generation of computer architecture, with the thirteen questions as lenses on it (`docs/PATH.md`).
- **Your stack is the second core visual system, after the dive.** Why: the dive shows how a real chip is; the stack
  shows the one you made, and it is designed to be as strong as the dive (prototype by Claude Fable 5.2,
  `docs/prototypes/stack/`).
- **Seven rungs, shared with the dive and the lattice:** atom, switch, gate, block, chip, machine, mind. Why: one
  ladder seen three ways, so the stack is not a second system; sums and memory are parts of the block, cores part of
  the chip.
- **A recipe of four genes is a set of forks in the stack.** *Grow an architecture* and *Your stack* become one model:
  the stack computes a recipe's anatomy, the architecture engine grows its maturity. Why: the next generation of
  architecture is the path seen from above.
- **Grow an architecture's own race and map of 400 retire into the stack world;** its two challenges, family tree,
  curve and keeping in the ledger stay. **The lattice becomes an arrangement of the same ladder,** not a separate
  picture. Why: the growth bar and the field already do both jobs; nothing shown twice.
- **Diversity by design:** the best per niche, never one leaderboard; an open genome, its public form only after the
  first forks carry; credit for trying, not only for winning; agents explore, people choose. Why: a field that keeps
  one champion collapses back onto today's computer; new ideas need a way in.
- **The need ("what it is for") sits at the top of the stack,** starts on *Any program*, and is not a forced first
  step. The owner had no preference; it stays open until the framework settles.
- **Fable's prototype is the reference to port in small steps** (`docs/PATH.md`, "Building it"), as task issues for
  the nightly build. Why: the design is decided in the prototype; the port follows the lab's rules (the atlas, words,
  styles, loading).
