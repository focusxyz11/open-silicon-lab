# Roadmap

Where Open Silicon Lab is heading. This is the one place for what happens next. Nothing here is a promise or a date. Ideas not yet picked are kept in the [idea bank](IDEAS.md).

## Now: the dive

The site is one thing: a continuous dive from a data centre, a phone, a car or a chip factory down to silicon atoms, with open questions running through it as threads ([FRAME.md](FRAME.md)). The earlier site is kept on the branch `claude/before-dive-2026-11`.

1. **Done:** the dive with threads, in Fable's style, as the whole site.
2. **Done:** different types of chips: dive into a data centre, a phone or a car; all three meet in the lower depths.
3. **Your chip** (in progress): one chip whose settings every *Try it* changes and every depth shows. Done: clock speed, switch size, chip size, and the factory as a way down, in 2.5D (its line, one layer's loop with a slice through the wafer, yield from your chip's size, laps from its switches, Build it at the switch). Next: the open chip as a way down (your square of a shared chip, with a RISC-V core that runs a real program), then the other settings, with the earlier site's simulations as their models.
4. **Try it with people.** Three to five learners, from a child to an engineer, while someone watches where they get stuck and which questions they follow.
5. **More questions,** first where few threads stop, then from the community. More ways down when they bring new questions.

## Next

- **Bring back what fits.** The earlier site's simulations become the models behind your chip's settings and tries, never separate pages.
- **The companion, live.** It now hands the visitor a prompt for any AI assistant; next, a conversation inside the lab where the page can reach an AI.
- **Other strong visual views** that connect with the dive.

## Long term

- **Learners become contributors:** questions, challenges and designs from the community, reviewed by people who know chips.
- **Plug in real hardware,** as a bonus, never a gate.
