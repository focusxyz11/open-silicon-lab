# Roadmap

Where Open Silicon Lab is heading. This is the one place for what happens next. Nothing here is a promise or a date. Ideas not yet picked are kept in the [idea bank](IDEAS.md).

## Now: the dive

The site is one thing: a continuous dive from a data centre down to silicon atoms, with open questions running through it as threads ([FRAME.md](FRAME.md)). The earlier site is kept on the branch `claude/before-dive-2026-11`.

1. **Done:** the dive with threads, in Fable's style, as the whole site.
2. **Try it with people.** Three to five learners, from a child to an engineer, while someone watches where they get stuck and which questions they follow.
3. **Different types of chips.** Dive into a phone, a car, a microcontroller, not only a data centre.
4. **More questions,** first where few threads stop, then from the community.

## Next

- **Bring back what fits.** Simulations and challenges from the earlier site, opened inside the dive where they belong, one at a time.
- **A companion** that does not answer, but asks the question behind the question.
- **Other strong visual views** that connect with the dive.

## Long term

- **Learners become contributors:** questions, challenges and designs from the community, reviewed by people who know chips.
- **Plug in real hardware,** as a bonus, never a gate.
