About this sketch. Everything you see is worked out by the lab's own tiny-chip engine as you change things: it reads the description, tries every input, chooses gates, places and wires them. Nothing is a drawing made by hand, and every sentence under the question that states a number reads it from the engine. It shows one idea from the framework: an experiment is a question, an engine and a way of seeing, and the picture and the words are the same thing. It is not the site: it has no 3D, it does not follow your design to the factory, and its words are in this file. The engine's figures are rough on purpose: they keep the direction and the size of an effect right, not the digits.
