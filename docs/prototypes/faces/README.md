# Three faces: the studio and the fab as faces of Your stack

A guiding prototype by Claude Fable 5.2 (27 November 2026), built on the Your stack prototype (`../stack/`). It shows the decision, not a finished build: every number in the faces is a stand-in, not the repository's studio or fab engines, and it still uses the prototype's eight rung names, not the seven of `docs/PATH.md`.

- **The idea:** the stack does not move. Along (← →, or the switch *As designed · As made · As used* under the rail) turns every plate at once. As designed (the studio), the plates are drafted dashed outlines with the studio's steps as their control; as made (the fab), they are solid with the line's steps as their control; as used is the stack as before (and the dive). The studio and the fab are two states of the same rungs, joined by the masks on the switch plate.
- **What the fab hands up:** the line's care sets the spread of the copies (σ) inside the carry and the dust per cm², so yield and the cost of a good chip show on the chip plate.
- **Arrange** on the chip plate as designed is the recipe's `shape` and `where` genes.
- New code: `src/faces.js`. Open `index.html`; rebuild from the repository's root with `npm install --no-save esbuild && node docs/prototypes/faces/build.mjs --min`.
