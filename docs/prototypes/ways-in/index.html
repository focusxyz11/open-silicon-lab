The ways in: three structures

For #524. Each option drawn at 1440 px and 390 px; the button switches day and night. What is lit blue is where you are; the warm column is the stage you would go to.

What is wrong now (docs/WAYS-IN.md): a stage of the loop has no fixed destination. alongTo turns a stack face, walks a line step, opens the bench or the dive at the level you hold, or lands on that stage's place on the island: the same button goes to five kinds of place, and the whole (the loop across, scale down, the stack as spine) is never shown before you are inside one part of it.

A. Three whole views in the bar; the Map is the home; the loop a lens inside each view

Trades: the same button always opens the same view, centred on where you are, and the Map shows the whole first; but the loop stops being a row in the bar, so a chip's life is read inside a view (the Map's columns, the island's road, the stack's faces) rather than stepped from anywhere.

B. Keep the loop in the bar, with one fixed destination per stage: its column on the Map

Trades: the smallest change, and the loop keeps its place as the main thread; every stage now does one thing (it opens the Map on its column, your level marked there), so nothing jumps into a place. But each step along costs a tap more to get back into a picture, and the island and the stack stay a step away from the bar.

C. The three views in the bar, and the loop as a quiet line under it, the next move shown before you take it

Trades: both the whole and the thread stay in sight: the switch says which whole you look through, the line says where you stand along a chip's life, and ← → names where it will land first. It is the busiest bar of the three, and on a phone the line shrinks to the stage alone.
