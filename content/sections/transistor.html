The transistor

A switch that must turn fully off

A transistor is a switch. Voltage on the gate lets current flow from source to drain through a channel. As channels shrank, the gate lost control: current leaked even when the switch was off, wasting power. Each new transistor shape wraps the gate around more of the channel to restore control.

Pick a transistor generation and sweep the gate voltage. The plot uses a log scale, so each gridline is ten times more current. A steeper curve means a cleaner switch.
