Where the money goes

An AI GPU sells for $30,000 to $40,000, yet costs only about $6,000 to manufacture. That gap pays for years of design work and software, and it is why the companies at the chokepoints are among the most profitable in the world. Follow the money from a single chip up to the hundreds of billions that cloud companies now spend each year.

Five ways to make money from chips

The industry splits into business models with very different economics. A fabless company designs chips and pays a foundry to make them. A foundry manufactures for others and must spend a third of its revenue on new factories. An integrated device manufacturer (IDM) does both. Memory makers swing from losses to record profits as prices rise and fall. An IP licensor sells designs and collects royalties without making anything. The price ladder

Chip economics spans ten orders of magnitude, from cents per transistor to hundreds of billions of dollars per year. Each step up the ladder is roughly ten times the one below. The bars use a log scale so all of them fit.

Could you start a chip company?

Hundreds of chip startups have been founded since 2016, many aimed at AI. Most of the money goes to engineers for two to three years before the first chip comes back from the fab. Choose a chip, a process node and a team, and see what it takes to reach first silicon. Try sharing a multi-project wafer with other companies, or using AI design agents.
