Packaging & memory

The package is now part of the chip

AI chips spend much of their time waiting for data. The fix is to put memory right beside the processor. High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) stacks DRAM dies on top of each other, joined by thousands of vertical through-silicon vias, and sits millimetres from the compute dies on a shared silicon interposer. TSMC's version of this is called CoWoS, and its capacity has been one of the tightest bottlenecks in the AI boom.
