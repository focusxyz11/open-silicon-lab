Tiny chip studio

Design a tiny chip

Take one small circuit through the same eight steps as a billion-transistor GPU: write the logic, prove it works, turn it into gates, lay it out on silicon, make masks, fabricate, test and ship. Every step uses the real method in miniature, and every step feeds the next, so a bug you miss early shows up later. Level 2 is the AI chip architect game. The tiny hardware language

              
a & bAND: 1 only if both are 1
a | bOR: 1 if either is 1
a ^ bXOR: 1 if they differ
~aNOT: flips 0 and 1
( )Group, as in algebra
w = …;Any new name is an internal wire
// noteA comment

              
Real chips are written in SystemVerilog or VHDL. This language keeps only the part that describes logic, so every line becomes real gates. Each line describes hardware that always exists, not steps that run one after another.
