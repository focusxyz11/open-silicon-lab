# Puzzles

A **puzzle** is one real problem that runs through every level of a machine and every stage of a chip's life, so no
single level can solve it. It is a new kind of open question for the lab, beside the three shapes of the questions
from the field (`src/data/questions.js`):

| Shape | What meets in it | Where it is met |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **limit** | one thing pushed to its edge | at one level |
| **leak** | two things, where one level hides what comes back through another | on a seam between two levels |
| **bet** | three things pulling at once, chosen without proof | at one level or place |
| **puzzle** | everything, from the hall to the atom | across every seam |

A puzzle is attacked by people and AI agents together, and **one shared score judges every attempt**. The score comes
from an engine, never from votes: an attempt is a plan the engine can run, and its score is what the engine says.
The best attempts usually cross levels: a materials idea, a memory trick and a better schedule each save a little
alone and a lot together, which is why a puzzle is worth solving as a crowd.

Each puzzle is one document with the same parts, in the shape of a world where it fits:

1. **The question**, and the question behind it.
2. **Why it is open**: the two true things that pull against each other.
3. **What is true**, with sourced figures.
4. **Level by level**: where the problem shows at each level, the levers people are trying there (with their catch),
   and the lab or field question already there.
5. **The engine** that scores an attempt.
6. **The score, and what an attempt is.**
7. **Where it meets other questions.**

Rules kept from the lab: a puzzle is never shown as a list or a menu page. A visitor meets it where they already are,
at a seam or a lab it runs through. "Puzzle" is a working name.
