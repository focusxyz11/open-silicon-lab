Running the fab

AI in scheduling, inspection and maintenance

TSMC is adopting NVIDIA AI for lithography, inspection and fab digital twins (vendor claim). Here AI makes the flow smoother: fewer surprises, so shorter queues, with no new machines. The size of the effect is illustrative.
